Restore my purchase

Bought Premium+ before, or got a new phone? Open the app, go to Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID (or Google account) you bought it with.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund help.

Deleting my data

Couplebait has no accounts. Your names and saved questions stay on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. See our Privacy Policy.

Suggest a question or report one

Have a great question idea, or found one that feels off? Email us. We read everything.
