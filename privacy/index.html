Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Couplebait (“we”, “us”) is a question game for couples. We built it to work without accounts and to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the Couplebait app and this website.

Short version

No account. No ads. No selling data. Your names, answers and saved questions stay on your device. The only data that leaves your phone is what's needed to process and restore your purchase.

Data stored only on your device

The names you type in, your saved (favorite) questions, your settings and which questions you have already seen are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data. Your answers are spoken out loud and are never recorded.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, email or payment details. To unlock and restore Premium+, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., a purchase‑management service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt and basic technical data (such as device type, OS version, app version, country/store and IP address) to verify the purchase. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

App updates and technical data

The app may download content updates (for example, new questions). Like any internet request, this involves your IP address and basic device information, which are used only to deliver the update. If the app crashes, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash reports with us if you have allowed this in your device settings.

What we don't do

We do not use advertising, ad tracking, or third‑party analytics. We do not sell or share personal data. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing a card

If you share a question card, it is created on your device and sent with your device's share sheet to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them when no longer needed.

Children

Couplebait is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to its processing. Because we hold almost no data, most of it can be deleted by deleting the app. For anything else, email support@couplebait.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
